# Lore: the Dark Dimension and the Champions' Game

Status: **built into the main story (2026-10-29): see main-story.md.** The rest is still ideas. Written down from the
user's brief (2026-10-19) so future updates (main quest, monsters, raids,
bosses, NPCs) can build toward it. Names marked TBD are still open.

## The core idea

The world has a mirror: a **Dark Dimension**, a shadowed version of the
normal world, in the spirit of the Upside Down in *Stranger Things*.

- For thousands of years it has been ruled by one being. **Name: TBD.**
- He is **dying**. He has lived thousands of years and is reaching the end of his life.
- When he dies, someone will take his place and rule the Dark Dimension.

This gives the world its pressing problem, and a reason for the monsters
and the main quest. Right now neither has one.

## The great powers and the vow

- All the **great powers of the normal world** want to be the one who takes his place.
- They are all very strong, and each knows the others are too.
- If two of them fought directly, the damage would be enormous and would
  **throw off the balance of the world**. It would also leave both open to a
  **third power stepping in** while they were weakened. It would be a mess.
- So they all took a **vow not to fight each other directly**.

## The Champions' Game

- Instead, **every twenty years each of the seven powers may summon one champion** (the Accord; updated 2026-10-29, was "each year").
- A power can teach its champion whatever it needs and use them to pursue its goals. Most take their new champion home to train (the Dragon King in the Deep Mountains, the Demon Lord on the Deep Continent), so the player meets their own year late.
- The game **started eighty years ago**: five summonings, the First Age to the Fifth (was "five years ago"). Fewer survive from each older age, and those are far stronger. The Accord itself is secret: the world knows the powers and some champions as heroes, and only the player's patron explains the Game, in cutscenes. See main-story.md.

## The player

- The player is the **fifth summon of one particular power**. (Which power: TBD.)
- **The first four summons were all wiped out.**
- Other champions from earlier years are still out there: the earlier
  "ranks" or "classes" of heroes, each serving their own power.

## Why it's interesting (what to build toward)

- **Hidden allegiances.** A big part of the fun is working out who serves
  whom: "Is this person an apostle of the Dragon God, or of the Dragon King?"
  NPCs, rivals, bosses and other champions can carry hints (symbols, words,
  powers, who they protect) that the player learns to read.
- **Senior champions.** Champions from earlier years are further along,
  stronger, and may be allies, rivals or enemies.
- **The four who died.** Their fate is a mystery and a thread for the main
  quest: who or what killed them, what they left behind, why the player's
  power keeps summoning.
- **A reason for the monsters.** The Dark Dimension leaking into the normal
  world as its ruler weakens explains wandering monsters, night raids on the
  village, dens, field bosses and discovery bosses.

## Ideas for hooking it into what already exists

Suggestions only; the user decides.

- The main quest already starts with a summoned stranger in Elder Glen ("A
  Stranger in Elder Glen"). That fits: the player is the summon.
- Night raids, dens and the new creatures can be tied to rifts or thin
  places where the Dark Dimension bleeds through.
- Discovery bosses could be champions of rival powers, or things from the
  Dark Dimension.
- The class mentors (Kaela, Corvin, Ilse, Magus Orren, Brother Aldous) could
  each have a quiet allegiance of their own.
