# The Lost Lanterns

Code: `src/world/lostLanterns.ts`. Saved in the world flags (`ll:<id>` lit,
`llTold` told of, `llHint:<id>` rumoured).

Before the Fall the keepers of the Dawn hung a lantern on every road and over
every door. When the Dark came they went out and were scattered. There are
about **720** of them: about 650 across every region of the world and about
70 in the Dark.

## Where they are

`placeLanterns()` places them from a fixed seed, so every world has the same
ones:

- **Elder Glen:** about 45 in the meadows, woods and river banks.
- **Every road:** one every few hundred metres, a little off the verge.
- **The sites of Cresha:** two in five have one tucked nearby.
- **Named places:** a few around every town, city, castle, port and ruin.
- **Every region:** 10 to 18 more, leaning toward high ground (the isles,
  the mountains, the desert, the Valorian Empire, the Demon Continent).
- **The Dark:** around the Gravewood's wheel, seen and lit only while you
  are in the Dark.

They keep clear of towns, camps, lairs, sites, roads and water, and trees
are not planted on them.

## How they sit

- **Post:** on a leaning wooden post.
- **Hang:** from the arm of a dead stump.
- **Nook:** in the corner of a broken wall.
- **Buried:** half sunk, on its side.
- **Cairn:** on a stack of stones, with a step stone to climb.
- **Tall:** on a 4 to 7 m pillar: an air jump, a glide from higher ground
  or the grappling hook.

You must reach the lantern itself (within 2.5 m). Unlit lanterns chime the
first time you come near and glimmer within 18 m. That is 36 m for a
Lightbinder, anyone with a light ability, or anyone carrying the Lantern-Glass
Charm, and 60 m on the Night of Lanterns.

## Relit

A relit lantern burns for good. Up close it flickers and lights the ground
by night. Far off, every lantern you have lit is a warm point on the land
(violet in the Dark), so the world fills with lights as you go.

## Telling the Temple

Brother Aldous (the Chapel of the Dawn) and High Priestess Seraphine (the
capital) take word of them. Every ten is **+2 health, +2 mana and +1
stamina** for good. The Lanternbearer titles come at 10, 30, 60, 100, 180,
280, 400 and 550.

## A lit region

Light every lantern of a region (or of the Dark), and **foes there hit you
15% more softly by night**.

## Finding them

- The Black Market's fence sells rumours: the nearest unlit lantern,
  marked on your map.
- The Almanac's Lost Lanterns page counts every region.
